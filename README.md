## Featured project: [quant-portfolio](https://github.com/lucianoblankf/quant-portfolio)

Nine self-contained projects in quantitative finance and econometrics on Brazilian market data. Each one implements the method from scratch, tests it against known answers, runs it on real data and reports what the results do and do not show, including the negative ones. Statistics and econometrics are in **R**; pricing, risk engines and machine learning are in **Python**.

| Project | What it shows |
|---------|---------------|
| [Returns and fat tails](https://github.com/lucianoblankf/quant-portfolio/tree/main/01-returns-fat-tails) | Ibovespa excess kurtosis of 12; a normal VaR is breached 1.9% of the time at 99% for USD/BRL |
| [Volatility and VaR backtesting](https://github.com/lucianoblankf/quant-portfolio/tree/main/02-volatility-var-backtesting) | GARCH-t passes the Kupiec and Christoffersen tests at 99%; historical and normal VaR fail |
| [The pre-fixed curve](https://github.com/lucianoblankf/quant-portfolio/tree/main/03-treasury-curve) | Bootstrapping with LTN and NTN-F, DV01 and key-rate DV01 |
| [PCA of the yield curve](https://github.com/lucianoblankf/quant-portfolio/tree/main/04-yield-curve-pca) | One factor explains 95% of the variance of yield changes |
| [Option pricing](https://github.com/lucianoblankf/quant-portfolio/tree/main/05-option-pricing) | Black-Scholes, Monte Carlo, binomial tree, delta hedging and a real SPY volatility smile |
| [Mini risk engine](https://github.com/lucianoblankf/quant-portfolio/tree/main/06-mini-risk-engine) | VaR and ES by four methods, component VaR, stress tests, backtest |
| [Cointegration and pairs trading](https://github.com/lucianoblankf/quant-portfolio/tree/main/07-pairs-cointegration) | An honest negative result: cointegration does not survive out of sample |
| [Selic, inflation and the Taylor rule](https://github.com/lucianoblankf/quant-portfolio/tree/main/08-selic-ipca-taylor) | HAC regression, structural breaks, a VAR written from scratch |
| [Machine learning vs HAR](https://github.com/lucianoblankf/quant-portfolio/tree/main/09-ml-volatility-forecast) | Trees do not beat a linear model for volatility forecasting and fail in the 2020 crash |
